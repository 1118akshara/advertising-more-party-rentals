A few useful things

Good to
know.

Every event is different. These are useful starting points; specific arrangements can be discussed once we know more about your brief.

The offering spans games and challenges, photo and video experiences, performers, music and lighting, food and tasting stations, immersive installations, skill-based activities, novelty services and large-scale attractions. Browse the ten categories above for the complete list of 100 named services.
The page is designed around a tailored enquiry rather than fixed bundles. Share your occasion, audience, priorities and venue and the team can discuss combinations for your brief, subject to confirmation.
Space, access, ceiling height, power, floor conditions and guest flow vary by attraction. Share venue details early so the requirements for selected experiences can be checked before anything is confirmed.
Logistics depend on the item, venue and event location. Delivery, setup, operating windows and collection arrangements should be confirmed as part of a project-specific proposal.
Some experiences require trained operators or dedicated crew; others may have different arrangements. Staffing and supervision requirements should be agreed for each selected service before booking.
Lead time varies with the event date, venue, equipment and performer requirements. Enquire as early as practical, especially for larger events or several coordinated attractions. Availability is confirmed individually.
Attractions must suit the venue and be operated with appropriate controls. Mechanical rides, inflatables, fire or aerial performance, drones, CO₂ effects and similar attractions may require professional supervision, permissions and additional safety planning. Requirements should be verified before confirmation.
